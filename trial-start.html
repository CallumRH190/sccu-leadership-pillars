Same laptop, same browser

Everything you enter lives in the browser on one laptop. Use the same one from start to finish, and open their link on it.

Don't clear your browsing history

That would wipe the session. When you finish, press Download a backup to keep a copy.

Nothing goes onto the internet

Names, scores and examples stay in your browser and theirs, and in the message they send you.

Hidden by design, not locked

Their scores stay off your screen until the reveal. The trial relies on both of you playing it straight. Logins come with the full version.
